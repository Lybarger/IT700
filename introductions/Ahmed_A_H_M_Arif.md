Microfluidic Electrochemical Sensor (MES) for Point of Need (POC) Detetction 

My research is highly experimental. I'm trying to develope MESs for POC detection and I use a diverse amount of electroanalytical techniques (viz. Cyclic Voltammetry, Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy, Choroamperometry etc.) for the sensor evaluation and transduction explanation.
